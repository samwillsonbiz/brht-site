THE FAIR QUESTIONS

OKAY, BUT…

Is it actually free?+

Yes. Beat the Patriarchy is free to play. Playing without ads is free, too. Choose the experience that suits you.

Do I have to watch ads?+

No. Ads are completely optional. Choose them to support the mission, or enjoy the game ad-free.

Can I play on my phone?+

Yes. Open the game in your phone’s browser and start playing. Add it to your Home Screen for easy access whenever you need a comeback.

Words or actions — what’s the difference?+

Clapback uses expressive speech and gestures. Brawl brings exaggerated cartoon strikes and effects. Both modes have equal power, and both let you build combos. Pick the style that suits your mood.

How does the game support women’s causes?+

Optional in-game ads generate revenue. We direct the profits toward women’s causes. You choose whether to play with ads or enjoy the game ad-free.

What’s the game’s vibe?+

Playful, satirical, and a little ridiculous. Take on familiar sexist attitudes through fictional opponents, expressive comebacks, and exaggerated cartoon action. Clapback gives you a words-only style.

Will my progress follow me to another device?+

Your progress is saved on the device and browser where you play. The game has backup and restore options in Settings. Keep using the same game address, and back up your progress before switching devices or clearing browser data.
