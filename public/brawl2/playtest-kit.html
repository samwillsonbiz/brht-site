Current rules

Build your fight

Choose any 24 cards from your Hero’s 40-card pool: 20 designs, two copies each. No point budget or Basic/Hero quotas. Choose one of three Fury cards; keep it outside the deck.

Start

20 Health, 0 Coins. Start on spaces 3 and 6; random Edge. Shuffle your deck and draw 7.

Commit

Secretly choose two Attack-capable cards in order. A Dual card uses one half only. If you have fewer than two Attacks, commit those you have; missing slots pass.

Reveal & speed

Flip the first pair. Apply printed adjacent speed reductions. Reveal payments are optional, offered in Edge order. Then lock attack order: higher Speed first; Edge wins ties. Movement cannot change that order.

Activate & react

Resolve printed preparation and movement. Then the defender reacts first. Alternate one card Reaction, Loot, token action or pass. Playing a card or token action resets passes; two consecutive passes resolve the strike at the current distance. Draw only when a card or passive says so.

Movement

Move up to the printed distance, in either direction. You may stay, move partly or cross the rival, but never share a space or leave the eight-space arena. Choose when the effect resolves. Push and pull also allow zero or partial movement.

Scry & preparation

Scry: privately look at the top cards, choose their order on top, and put unselected cards underneath. No draw. Alf’s preparation then publicly reveals and bottoms the top card: Attack-capable gives +2 damage and ignores 2 Block; otherwise minimum Range becomes 1 and maximum Range gains 2.

Payments & discards

Printed reaction costs are paid before effects. Discard another means a different uncommitted hand card. An opponent forced to discard chooses their own card. Optional pre-reaction Health payments are spent even if the attack later misses. All Health costs leave at least 1 Health.

Card draw & recovery

Only one printed draw effect per player per active attack. Explicit draws may exceed 7. Recover only the specified Basic Reaction already played in this exchange; never recover a committed Attack or a prior exchange’s card.

Finish the round

Finish both attacks in the first pair, then reveal and resolve pair two. Keep unused cards. Draw up to 4, stopping at 7 in hand. No free discard. Refill an empty deck by shuffling your discard. Edge alternates.

Win

Reduce the rival to 0 Health. A lethal effect immediately resolves any pending already-active Helga reflection, then victory. Skip all remaining effects, including on-hit healing, Exploding Present self-loss and rewards. Lethal Loot also ends immediately. Simultaneous knockouts draw. At round 10 cleanup both lose 2 Health, then one more per later round, capped at 18.

Fury & Last Stand

Health damage and Health payments both count. The first time you survive at 10 Health or less, add your chosen Fury to hand; commit it in a future round. At 5 Health or less, finish the triggering card; if alive, activate Last Stand permanently. Healing does not remove either unlock. A lethal hit unlocks neither. Already-active Helga reflection resolves before victory.

Coins

Basic Attacks earn 1 Coin when they finish, even on a miss. Franc also gains 1 Profit on a damaging Basic Attack and may immediately cash 1 for 1 Coin. Reactions give no automatic income. Hold at most 8 Coins.

Arena

Join the two Heroes’ four-space boards to make a straight 8-space arena. Number the spaces 1–8. Start on 3 and 6. Only the outer ends (1 and 8) are walls. Crossing the join between 4 and 5 has no special effect. Pushing beyond an outer wall deals 1 wall damage, ignoring Block. Smasherton may add Crunch when eligible.

Loot draft & market

Each Hero chooses 6 different Loot from their 12-card pool. Shuffle both contributions into one shared 12-card deck. Reveal 3. Anyone can use any Loot. Between rounds, starting with the new Edge holder, each player may buy once: pay 2 Coins for a face-up Loot or a blind draw. Refill a bought slot immediately. Hold at most 2 Loot. Shuffle spent Loot when the draw pile empties; never shuffle held or face-up cards. Franc may first use Inside Deal once per shopping turn to buy a face-up item with 2 Profit, then take his normal Coin purchase if he has room.

Block

All unused Block clears after every attack, for everyone. Maximum 6 Block. Helga can break Armour for Block; she no longer receives free Block at enemy activation.

Hero tokens

Tokens replace the old passive abilities. They work from the start and persist between rounds. Follow the Hero board for earning and spending. A token reaction passes priority like a card. Activation choices happen before printed movement. Fury and Last Stand do not spend tokens.

Hit & modifiers

An attack connects if its current Range includes the rival. On hit requires at least 1 Health damage from that attack. Block prevents damage and is spent; ignored Block remains until cleanup, while removed Block is destroyed immediately. Damage, Range, piercing and other attack modifiers last only for the current attack. Loot damage is not an attack hit.

Attack cleanup

If no lethal effect ended the fight, resolve on-hit effects and each delayed per-card effect, then already-active Helga reflection and victory. If still fighting, settle pending Last Stands and attack-end token effects. Discard the finished attack. Clear both players’ unused Block and temporary attack modifiers before the next attack. Smasherton’s pin ends when the pinned fighter finishes their next attack.

Wall slam

A push beyond space 1 or 8 stops at the wall and deals 1 damage ignoring Block. Landing exactly on the wall deals no wall damage. One push effect causes at most one slam. Combine printed attack push and on-hit bonus push. Pulls never slam. Smasherton may spend Impact for Crunch only on an attack wall slam.

Draw limits

At most one printed draw effect per player per active attack, including attack activation and on-hit draws. Aim and Trick draws use that allowance. Shealer’s immediate Research draw after paying Health is a separate token reward, not a printed draw effect. Fury entering hand is also separate. Loot never counts against the seven-card refill limit.

Pin and swaps

Pin prevents voluntary Move, adjacent repositioning, token movement, Shadow swaps and ordinary swaps initiated by the pinned fighter. A blocked swap skips only the relocation; its other effects still resolve. A rival’s swap forcibly relocates a pinned target. Push and pull are forced movement and still work unless separately resisted. Swaps do not earn Raven Tricks.

Repeated delayed effects

Each Exploding Present played registers its own 1-Health loss after a damaging strike, unless an earlier lethal effect has ended the fight. Each Hold Your Ground registers its own Coin reward, capped by the shared 8-Coin limit. Any Health damage from the active attack, including its wall slam, prevents these rewards. Separate Loot damage does not.
