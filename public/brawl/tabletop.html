FABLE FURY: BRAWL · v0.8

Two actions. One clear rhythm.

18 HP · 0 starting Coins · 3-Coin Loot · hold at most 2

  1. BUY Each player may buy one blind Loot.
  2. PLAN Secretly choose two combat cards, in order.
  3. REVEAL Reveal both first cards together.
  4. LOOT Initiative player attaches up to one, then the rival. Apply On attach effects immediately.
  5. RESOLVE Compare Speed; Initiative breaks ties. Each player resolves before-Action Loot → Action → after-Action Loot.
  6. CLEAR Finish end-of-reveal effects. Clear this reveal effects. Repeat steps 3–6 for the second pair.
  7. CLEANUP Arena Fury if due → discard played cards and used Loot → refill → clear this round effects → pass Initiative.

Move when it resolves. Choose a legal destination. “Up to” and “may” allow zero. Crossing costs the space you pass through; never stop on a rival.

Two durations. This reveal: both current Actions. This round: both reveals. Helga’s Ultimate is the explicit carry-over exception.

Use the cards as reminders. Leave Actions in their slots. Turn a pending bonus sideways, then straighten it when spent. Keep active Loot visible.

Refill, never discard down. Draw to 5, or 6 if Flurry earned it. Keep cards already above that number. A newly unlocked Fury Skill is extra for its first planning opportunity.

One Loot window. Only after each reveal. No Events, interrupt windows or third Actions. A KO cancels unresolved effects.

Arena Fury. At round 13 cleanup, both lose 2 HP; +2 each later round. No defence, Fury or Pain. Double knockout = draw.

Complete rules

Build your fighter

Choose 10 Basics and 5 Hero cards, with up to 2 copies of each design. Or randomize a deck and edit or reroll it. Start at 18 HP, 0 Coins, spaces 2 and 6. Draw 5. There is no Event deck.

One round · two actions each

  1. Purchase: each player may buy one random secret Loot for 3 Coins. Hold at most 2.
  2. Program: secretly choose two combat cards in order.
  3. Reveal: turn over both Action 1 cards together.
  4. Attach Loot: Initiative player may attach one, then the opponent may attach one. Apply effects labelled “On attach” immediately. This is the only Loot window.
  5. Order and resolve: compare effective Speed. Higher goes first unless Alarm Clock reverses it; Initiative breaks ties. Resolve that player’s before-Action Loot, whole Action card, then after-Action Loot. Resolve the other player the same way. A KO ends combat and cancels unresolved effects.
  6. End reveal: resolve printed end-of-reveal effects, then clear “this reveal” effects. Repeat steps 3–6 for Action 2.
  7. Cleanup: apply Arena Fury if due, discard played cards and used Loot, refill to 5 (6 if Flurry earned it), keeping cards already above that target. Clear “this round” effects and pass Initiative.

Just two effect durations

This reveal lasts through both players’ current Actions, then expires. This round lasts through both reveals, then expires at cleanup. Defensive Loot activates on attachment and lasts this reveal; it never waits until a future Action. Keep active Loot beside its Action until the effect expires. Helga’s Ultimate explicitly carries up to 2 unused Block into the next round.

For tabletop play, keep played cards in their two slots until cleanup. Turn a card sideways while its pending bonus remains; straighten it when spent. Keep the Hero reference visible. No extra status tokens or Event phase are required.

Damage and resources

Basics earn 1 Coin after resolving, even on a miss. Battle Cry earns 2 total. Coin cap 8. Hero cards and Skills earn no automatic Coins. Each opponent Action card or Loot card causing HP loss gives at most 1 Fury, including multiple hits and Wall Slams. Fury is never spent: at 3, add your Skill to hand once; after use it cycles normally. At 5, activate your Ultimate after the effect finishes if you survive.

Spend Block point for point, using this-reveal Block before this-round Block. Ignored Block remains. Dodge cancels one Attack and its on-hit effects; it does not cancel a printed after-Attack movement. On-hit means actual HP loss. Card-wide bonuses apply to the first Attack only. Healing cannot exceed 18 HP. Direct HP loss ignores Block and Dodge; self-damage grants no Fury unless stated.

Movement happens when it resolves

Click a highlighted arena space. “Up to” and “may” allow zero; mandatory movement requires moving if possible. Each space crossed costs 1, including the rival’s space. You may cross but cannot finish on them. Grappling Hook lets you click the rival’s destination to Pull 0, 1 or 2. Fighters automatically face each other; there are no turn actions.

Voluntary crossing primes Raven’s Backstab or Nothing Personal this round. The bonus is consumed when used. Forced movement does not prime it. Push or Pull beyond an active wall causes 1 HP loss at most once per effect. Pinwheel and Closing Walls relocate without Wall Slams or crossing bonuses.

Private information

A peek reveals only the inspected card. If Crystal Ball replaces it, the new card is secret. Local players use the privacy screen for hidden choices. Click a Hero portrait for their trait, Skill and Ultimate.

Arena Fury

At the end of round 13, both lose 2 HP simultaneously, rising by 2 each later round. It ignores defence and grants no Fury or Pain. A double knockout is a draw.

Physical setup

Each player needs their own pool of two copies of every Basic. Choose different Heroes unless you have duplicate Hero and Fury sets. Use a shared 25-card Loot deck. v0.8 removes Chaotic Arena and folds its ideas into Loot. Start a new match or room for these rules.

Hero field guide

Lord Smasherton

Close-range bruiser / berserker / wall pressure

Pain Is Power: each effect causing HP loss gives 1 Pain, up to 2 gained per round. Your next Attack consumes all Pain for that much bonus damage, even if it misses. Self-damage grants no Fury unless printed.

3 Fury — Unstoppable Charge: Move up to 3 spaces, then Attack for 3 damage at Range 1. Push 1 on hit. Pain applies to this Attack.

5 Fury — Closing Walls: Remove the leftmost and rightmost active battlefield spaces. Heroes occupying a removed space relocate to the nearest legal active space.

Raven Madison

Mobile assassin / cross-up / backstab

Hold 1 Dodge. Spend it to cancel one Attack and all its on-hit effects. After that Attack, you may Move 1; your next Attack this round gets +1 damage and ignores 1 additional Block. Unused Dodge expires at cleanup. Crossing the opponent primes Backstab this round. Backstab or Nothing Personal consumes the crossing; forced movement does not prime it.

3 Fury — Nothing Personal: Move up to 2 spaces, then Attack for 2 damage at Range 1–2. If you crossed the opponent this round, add +1 damage and consume that crossing. Set Dodge.

5 Fury — Living Shadow: Once per round, after voluntarily crossing the opponent, Set Dodge and prime +1 damage for your next Backstab this round. No facing bookkeeping.

Alf Featherbottom

Long-range archer / information control / zoning

Aim - Alf may hold 1 Aim. At the end of a reveal, if Alf did not move or get moved, gain Aim. Before one of his Attacks resolves, spend Aim to increase maximum Range by 1, or deal +2 damage if the opponent is currently at Range 3 or farther.

3 Fury — Eagle Eye: Gain Aim, then you may Move up to 2 spaces. Scout the opponent’s unrevealed Action, or 2 random hand cards. Attack for 2 damage at Range 2–5, ignoring 1 Block. You may spend Aim normally.

5 Fury — No Safe Distance: For the rest of combat, your ranged Attacks ignore maximum Range (minimum Range still applies). Your first ranged Attack each round also ignores 2 Block.

Friar Franc

Combo fighter / Fury engine / resource pressure

Discipline: resolve a Basic as Action 1 to give a Hero card (including your unlocked Skill) in Action 2 +1 Speed and +1 damage on its first Attack only. If both Action cards cause HP loss, gain 1 Fury after Action 2, once per round. Loot damage does not qualify.

3 Fury — Flurry of Faith: You may Move 1, then Attack twice for 2 damage at Range 1. If both cause HP loss, refill to 6 at this cleanup. Card-wide bonuses affect the first Attack only.

5 Fury — Divine Rhythm: For the rest of combat, your first hit each round primes your next Action card this round: +1 Speed and +1 damage to its first Attack. It must be a later Action card, not a later hit of the same card. One step per round; Loot cannot trigger or consume it.

Helga Stonewall

Tank / counterattack / positional control

Resolve: hold 1. Once per round, after your Block prevents at least 2 total Attack damage (including across multiple Attacks), gain Resolve. Before an Attack deals damage, spend Resolve for +2 damage and +1 Push.

3 Fury — Shield Wall: Gain 5 Block this round. If an Attack at Range 1 is fully stopped by your Block this reveal, deal 2 damage back to its attacker. Each stopped Attack can trigger this once.

5 Fury — ABSOLUTELY NOT: Immediately gain 2 Block this round. At cleanup, keep your unused Block, to a maximum of 2, for the next round. It remains a finite pool, not added to a fresh pool.

Dr. Shealer

Medic / preparation / surgical burst

Hold up to 3 Remedies. Remedies - after one of Shealer’s Hero cards or Hero Skill with printed Block/Healing resolves, gain 1 Remedy, max once per card. Before one of her cards resolves, spend at most 1 Remedy to Move 1 before resolving, add +1 Block if it has Block, or add +2 damage if it is an Attack.

3 Fury — Emergency Treatment: Heal 2 HP and gain 2 Block this reveal. Gain exactly 1 Remedy total from this card, including your passive.

5 Fury — Miracle Worker: Immediately Heal 2 HP and gain 1 Remedy. For the rest of combat, the first time each round a Hero Action card (including your Skill) actually heals you, gain 1 additional Remedy (max 3). Basic cards and Loot do not trigger this bonus.
