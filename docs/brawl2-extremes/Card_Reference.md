# Brawl2 — Distinct by design

Release: brawl2-extremes-1. 20 November 2026.

## Rules

### Build your fight

Choose any 24 cards from your Hero’s 40-card pool: 20 designs, two copies each. No point budget or Basic/Hero quotas. Choose one of three Fury cards; keep it outside the deck.

### Start

20 Health, 0 Coins. Start on spaces 3 and 5; random Edge. Shuffle your deck and draw 7.

### Commit

Secretly choose two Attack-capable cards in order. A Dual card uses one half only. If you have fewer than two Attacks, commit those you have; missing slots pass.

### Reveal & speed

Flip the first pair. Apply printed adjacent speed reductions. Reveal payments are optional, offered in Edge order. Then lock attack order: higher Speed first; Edge wins ties. Movement cannot change that order.

### Activate & react

Resolve printed preparation and movement. Then the defender reacts first. Alternate one Reaction, Loot or pass. Playing a card resets passes; two consecutive passes resolve the strike at the current distance. Never draw automatically during an exchange.

### Movement

Move up to the printed distance, in either direction. You may stay, move partly or cross the rival, but never share a space or leave the seven-space arena. Choose when the effect resolves. Push and pull also allow zero or partial movement.

### Block & damage

Block caps at 6. Damage consumes Block unless ignored. Ignore X bypasses X Block without consuming it. Ignore all bypasses all. Clear Block after each attack, except Helga banks up to 3 (5 when upgraded). All Block clears at round end.

### Coins & Loot

Basic Attacks earn 1 Coin when finished, even on a miss; Franc earns 2. No automatic Coins for reactions or Hero attacks, but printed effects can grant them. Coins cap at 8. End of round: buy one blind Loot for 2 Coins; hold at most 2.

### Scry & preparation

Scry: privately look at the top cards, choose their order on top, and put unselected cards underneath. No draw. Alf’s preparation then publicly reveals and bottoms the top card: Attack-capable gives +2 damage and ignores 2 Block; otherwise minimum Range becomes 1 and maximum Range gains 2.

### Payments & discards

Printed reaction costs are paid before effects. Discard another means a different uncommitted hand card. An opponent forced to discard chooses their own card. Optional pre-reaction Health payments are spent even if the attack later misses. All Health costs leave at least 1 Health.

### Card draw & recovery

Only one printed draw effect per player per active attack. Explicit draws may exceed 7. Recover only the specified Basic Reaction already played in this exchange; never recover a committed Attack or a prior exchange’s card.

### Finish the round

Finish both attacks in the first pair, then reveal and resolve pair two. Keep unused cards. Draw up to 4, stopping at 7 in hand. No free discard. Refill an empty deck by shuffling your discard. Edge alternates.

### Fury

At 10 Health or less, your chosen Fury enters your hand once; commit it in a future round. At 5 Health or less, upgrade your Hero ability permanently. Healing does not undo unlocks.

### Win

Reduce the rival to 0 Health; stop immediately. At round 10 cleanup both lose 2 Health, then one more per later round, capped at 18. Simultaneous knockouts are a draw.
